ESG Frameworks We Support

Comprehensive advisory and technical reporting aligned with global sustainability standards, regulatory mandates, and impact frameworks.

GRI

Global Reporting Initiative

The global common language for impact reporting. It evaluates an organization’s broad material impacts on the economy, environment, and society, ensuring complete stakeholder transparency.

SEBI BRSR

Business Responsibility & Sustainability Reporting

Mandatory for top Indian listed entities. Structures disclosures across 9 core ESG principles, covering comprehensive GHG footprints, workforce welfare, and value chain governance.

ISSB

International Sustainability Standards Board

Establishes a baseline of global disclosure standards (IFRS S1 & S2) focused on delivering decision-useful, financially material sustainability and climate-related risks to global investors.

CSRD

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive

Rigorous European Union mandate requiring strict compliance under the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) applying a double-materiality perspective.

TCFD

Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures

Framework structured around four core elements: Governance, Strategy, Risk Management, and Metrics & Targets to quantify financial exposure to physical and transition climate risks.

CDP

Carbon Disclosure Project

The gold standard for environmental disclosure. We assist organizations in scoring high on Climate Change, Water Security, and Forests questionnaires for global institutional investors.

SASB Standards

Sustainability Accounting Standards Board

Industry-specific standards identifying financially material sustainability factors across 77 distinct industries, connecting operational sustainability directly to enterprise value.

UN SDGs

Sustainable Development Goals

Maps corporate sustainability strategies directly onto the United Nations' 17 global goals, measuring and validating long-term corporate contributions toward global sustainable development.
