About Us

Vashisth Sustainability Consulting helps organizations integrate ESG, sustainability reporting, carbon accounting, climate risk assessment, and regulatory compliance into their business strategy.

Our mission is to support businesses in creating long-term value through responsible environmental, social, and governance practices.

About Leadership

The leadership combines a strong engineering foundation with extensive experience in ESG research, sustainability reporting, and data-driven analysis. Supported by specialized technical certifications in Python Programming from NIELIT and Geoprocessing using Python from IIRS–ISRO, the leadership integrates technical expertise with globally recognized sustainability frameworks, including GRI, BRSR, ISSB, CSRD, CDP, SASB, TCFD, UN SDGs, and other leading ESG standards.

With expertise in corporate sustainability, carbon accounting, climate risk assessment, ESG data analytics, and regulatory compliance, the firm helps organizations transform complex sustainability requirements into practical, data-driven business strategies. By combining analytical precision, automation, and digital innovation, Vashisth Sustainability Consulting enables businesses to strengthen ESG performance, improve regulatory readiness, and create long-term sustainable value.

In addition to sustainability advisory, the firm also provides professional website development and digital solutions, helping organizations establish a modern, credible, and impactful online presence.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the typical duration for completing a BRSR or GRI report?

The timeline varies depending on the organization's scale, data readiness, and complexity. We follow a structured phased approach—from initial data assessment to final disclosure drafting—ensuring complete accuracy and audit-readiness at every step.

2. Do you help with Scope 3 supply chain carbon accounting?

Yes, we provide robust calculation methods for all 15 categories of Scope 3 emissions aligned with the GHG Protocol.

3. How does data automation fit into your consulting services?

We leverage advanced data workflows and python-backed analytics to minimize human error and automate repetitive sustainability data tracking.
